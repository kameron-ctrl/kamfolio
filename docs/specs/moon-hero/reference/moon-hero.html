What makes it look 3D

The moon is a real sphere in a fixed WebGL canvas, sized and placed from an invisible box under the headline. These are the same proportions as the nasaforce.gov hero, rebuilt from scratch with your textures.

Scale
Radius is 30% of the box height (50% on phones). The box is full width and 1.2× as tall, so the sphere is wider than the screen and only its top cap shows.
Camera
A 45° lens two units from a sphere almost one unit in radius. Being that close bends the horizon and makes tilting read as real depth.
Light
Mostly backlit: a dim front fill brings out the craters from your height map, and a bright limb wraps the edge.
Gaze
The sphere tilts toward your cursor and eases there. Quick sideways moves add a small roll.
Scroll
As the box passes through the screen, the moon drifts slower than the page, a diagonal shadow lifts off its face, it tips forward, and the halo brightens.
